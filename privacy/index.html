1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Legal Underground (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit the Platform, create an account, build a profile, browse or post opportunities, submit applications, or otherwise interact with our services.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform. Our Terms of Service govern your use of the Platform and are incorporated by reference.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address, password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), display name, and account type (student or organization).
  • Student profile information: name, academic year, major, interests, short bio, and résumé link (typically a URL to an external document such as Google Drive).
  • Organization profile information: organization name, website, contact email, and description of the organization's work.
  • Opportunity listings: job titles, descriptions, locations, compensation details, requirements, and related posting metadata submitted by organizations.
  • Applications: applicant name, email, year, major, résumé link, application note, application status, and timestamps.
  • Communications: information you send us by email or other channels when you contact our team.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Platform, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical data, such as:

  • Device and browser type, operating system, and language settings;
  • IP address and general location derived from IP (city/region level);
  • Pages viewed, features used, referring URLs, and dates/times of access;
  • Authentication session tokens and error logs needed to operate and secure the Platform.

2.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not require Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, financial account numbers, or sensitive health information to use the Platform. Please do not include such information in profiles, applications, or messages unless an organization explicitly and lawfully requests it outside the Platform.

3. How we use information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts and authenticating sign-ins;
  • Operating the opportunity board, application workflow, and user dashboards;
  • Displaying student profiles to organizations when students apply to their listings;
  • Displaying organization profiles and listings to students and the public;
  • Coordinating weekly outreach to partner organizations regarding new applications (manual team process);
  • Screening and moderating listings and accounts to maintain platform quality and safety;
  • Responding to inquiries, troubleshooting, and providing user support;
  • Monitoring usage, diagnosing technical issues, and improving the Platform;
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms;
  • Complying with applicable law, lawful requests, and enforcing our policies.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use student application data for third-party advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

4. How we share information

4.1 With other users (core platform function)

The Platform is designed to connect students and organizations. When you apply to an opportunity, the posting organization receives the application information you submit (including profile fields attached to your application). Organization profiles and opportunity listings are visible to users browsing the Platform. Student profiles are visible to signed-in users as needed for the application process.

4.2 With service providers

We use trusted third-party providers to host and operate the Platform, including:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and optionally Analytics) — stores account, profile, listing, and application data;
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the public website files;
  • Google Fonts — delivers web fonts (your browser may send standard request data to Google).

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We configure services to collect only what is reasonably necessary to operate the Platform.

4.3 For legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to protect the rights, property, or safety of Legal Underground, our users, UCLA community members, or the public; or to investigate suspected fraud, harassment, or Terms violations.

4.4 Organizational change

If Legal Underground transfers platform operations to another student organization or successor group, user information may transfer as part of that transition, subject to notice where required by law.

5. Cookies & analytics

The Platform uses browser local storage and similar technologies to maintain demo-mode sessions when Firebase is not configured, and Firebase Authentication uses cookies/local storage to keep you signed in.

If Firebase Analytics is enabled, Google may collect usage analytics (such as page views and device information) to help us understand how the Platform is used. You can limit analytics through browser settings, ad/tracking controls, or browser extensions. Disabling cookies or storage may affect sign-in and dashboard functionality.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Platform, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Application records may be retained after a listing closes so students and organizations can reference prior interactions.

You may request account deletion by contacting us (see Section 12). Upon verified deletion requests, we will delete or anonymize personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law, needed to resolve disputes, or necessary for legitimate security and backup purposes.

7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including access controls, Firebase security rules, and HTTPS delivery where supported by our host. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password and for activity under your account. Notify us promptly at legalunderground@g.ucla.edu if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Your privacy rights

8.1 Access, correction, and deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you. You can update much of your profile directly in your dashboard. For other requests, email us with enough detail to verify your identity.

8.2 California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, including the right to know categories of personal information collected, the right to delete personal information (subject to exceptions), and the right to correct inaccurate personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under California law. To exercise rights, contact us at legalunderground@g.ucla.edu. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

8.3 Educational records

Information you choose to provide about your academic status (such as year or major) is supplied voluntarily for recruiting purposes and is not maintained by UCLA through this Platform. This Policy does not govern UCLA's official student records.

9. Children's privacy

The Platform is intended for college-level students and adult organizations. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Third-party services & external links

Opportunity listings, résumé links, organization websites, and our official club site may lead to third-party websites or services that we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for the content or data practices of partner organizations or external sites.

Firebase/Google privacy information: Firebase Privacy and Google Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice on the Platform. Continued use after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
